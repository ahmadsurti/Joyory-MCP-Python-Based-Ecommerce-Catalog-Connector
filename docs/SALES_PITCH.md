## 1. The Elevator Pitch

> *"Every month, millions of Indian consumers ask ChatGPT and Claude for skincare routines, makeup shades, and grooming advice. But today, those AI assistants are completely blind to live e-commerce inventory—recommending discontinued products, fabricating prices, and stranding buyers without purchase links.*  
>  
> *The **Joyory MCP Connector** fixes this. In less than 1,000 lines of high-performance code, we turn Joyory's live product catalog into an open, AI-queryable conversational commerce engine. Shoppers get instant, clinical-grade product consultations in natural language, while Joyory captures high-intent, pre-qualified traffic directly at the moment of discovery."*

## 3. What Makes This Different? (The Paradigm Shift)

### The Legacy Approach: The "Embedded Chatbot" Widget
* Retailers spend \$50,000+ building proprietary chatbot widgets (Intercom, Ada, custom React iframes) pinned to the bottom-right corner of their website.
* **The Flaw:** Customers have to already be on the website, wait for slow widgets to load, and navigate robotic decision trees.

### The Agentic Approach: The Open MCP Connector
* We meet consumers where they already spend their time: inside Claude, ChatGPT, and AI workspaces.
* **The Advantage:** When a user asks Claude: *"What should I buy for my dry, sun-damaged skin?"*, Claude natively accesses Joyory’s catalog, recommends verified products, and hands the shopper directly to the checkout page.

## 4. Commercialization & Revenue Models

### Model 1: B2B Enterprise SaaS for D2C Brands
* **Offering:** Turnkey "MCP-as-a-Service" for Indian beauty and lifestyle brands (Nykaa, Purplle, Tira, Sugar Cosmetics).
* **Packaging:** Tiered monthly subscription based on catalog size and query volume (e.g. ₹15,000/month for up to 10,000 catalog items).

### Model 2: Affiliate & Performance Attribution
* **Offering:** Dynamic affiliate tracking embedded within canonical product URLs (`https://joyory.com/product/xyz?ref=mcp_claude`).
* **Economics:** 8%–15% commission on finalized e-commerce gross merchandise value (GMV).

### Model 3: Sponsored Merchandising & Priority Discovery
* **Offering:** Brand-sponsored placement in search rankings for generic queries (e.g., when a user asks for *"best vitamin C serum"*, featured brand partners matching clinical criteria receive top ranking with an explicit sponsored disclosure).

## 5. Adoption & Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

1. **Phase 1 (Developer & Early Adopter Distribution):** Publish the connector to official MCP registries (Smithery.ai, mcp.so, awesome-mcp-servers).

3. **Phase 3 (Multi-Store Expansion):** Extend the underlying discovery and normalization adapter architecture to Shopify and WooCommerce stores across India.
